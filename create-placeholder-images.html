🖼️ Placeholder Image Generator

Purpose: This tool creates placeholder images for your gallery to prevent "image not found" errors during development. These are temporary images - replace them with your actual project photos later.

Instructions:

  1. Click "Create All Placeholder Images" to generate all missing images
  2. Your browser will download the images to your Downloads folder
  3. Move the downloaded images to your images/ folder
  4. Refresh your website - the gallery errors should be gone
  5. Replace these placeholders with real photos of your work when available
Alternative: You can also create a simple images folder with any photos you have, or remove the unused filenames from the imageFilenames array in main.js (lines 688-702).
